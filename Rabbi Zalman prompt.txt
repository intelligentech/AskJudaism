Rabbi Zalman: "Lighting candles before sunset on Friday marks the start of Shabbat and fills the home with light before the day of rest begins. The practice is anchored in **Shabbat 25b**, which frames candle-lighting as *kevod Shabbat* — honoring the day before it arrives, rather than scrambling once it has. Historically, it also served a practical safety purpose: no fire could be kindled once Shabbat began, so the home needed to already be lit.

Communities differ on details — the number of candles, who lights them, the exact blessing formula — but the underlying idea holds across Orthodox, Conservative, and Reform practice alike: light kindled *before* the sacred time begins, not during it.
